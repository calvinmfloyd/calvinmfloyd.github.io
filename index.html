ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Calvin Floyd! Most people call me Cal. I have a passion for problem solving and utilizing data to improve decision making and processes. I'm always searching for ways to become a more well-rounded data scientist - including sharpening my HTML and CSS skills with this personal website!

Currently, I'm a Data Engineering Manager working for Teamworks Intelligence. Previously, I was a Data Engineer with Zelus Analytics and a Data Scientist with the Sacramento Kings. Before that, I received a bachelors degree in mathematics from SUNY Geneseo and a masters degree in Applied and Computational Mathematics from Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT). RIT's College of Science actually did a profile on me in 2021!

My main hobbies include video games, puzzles, TV shows, movies and both playing and watching sports. I've played basketball my entire life and, growing up in upstate NY, I'm a huge fan of Syracuse Orange basketball and the Buffalo Bills. Overall, I like to try out new things to see if they stick or not.
